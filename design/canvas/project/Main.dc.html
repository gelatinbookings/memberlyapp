Memberly on the phone, with Gelatin talent built in

One native app for members and organizers. Booking a DJ is a section of Create New Event, not a separate marketplace. Draft for discussion, 30 Oct 2026. Every DJ, draw, member count and dollar figure on this canvas is sample data.

Flutter · iPhone first Dark appearance

Who owns what

Gelatin provides
  • Bookable DJ catalog: availability, rates, tiers, genres, draw
  • Discovery by city, date, budget, genre; alternates and waitlist
  • Booking engine: requests, offers, DJ notifications
  • Payments: server totals, checkout, card hold, capture, release, payouts
  • Portal in Memberly's theme for everything after first payment
  • Emails to organizers and all messages to DJs
  • Partner API, spec and TypeScript SDK
Memberly provides
  • The product, its organizers and audiences
  • Communities, events, members, passes, pass revenue
  • Every native screen: Lineup, browse, offer, hold, status, public lineup
  • A backend that holds the partner key and proxies every call
  • Organizer identity and event data: name, times, time zone, place
  • Portal theme and a registered return address
  • Notices to pass holders when a date changes

How it is wired

Memberly app
Flutter, iPhone and Android
Memberly backend
Holds the partner key, proxies every call
Gelatin
Partner API, booking engine, payments
Card hold
Gelatin's checkout page opens in a native in-app browser sheet. Apple Pay and Google Pay work there. Nothing is captured until the DJ accepts.
After the first payment
Counters, new prices, changes and refunds happen in Gelatin's portal, opened with a single-use link and returning to the app.
The app reads booking status by asking Memberly's backend every 5 seconds, only while a DJ is deciding. No keys, no secrets and no Gelatin call ever live in the app.
