# Icons

One stroke set, Lucide (`lucide-react-native`), always at a 2px stroke. Icons take their colour from a theme role: `foreground` by default, `mutedText` for chevrons and inactive tabs, `brandText` on the tint, `onBrand` on purple.

```tsx
import { ChevronRight } from 'lucide-react-native';

<ChevronRight size={16} color={c.mutedText} strokeWidth={2} />
```

## Rules

- An icon-only control needs a spoken name: `IconButton` requires `accessibilityLabel`.
- Decorative icons beside a label add nothing for VoiceOver; the label carries the meaning.
- Third-party marks (Google, Apple) are drawn as their owners require, from `brandMarks`, and are not icons.
- Don't fill icons, change the stroke, or use emoji in their place.
