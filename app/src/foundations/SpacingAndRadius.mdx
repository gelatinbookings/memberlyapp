## Rules

- **The Two-Radius Rule.** Primary buttons, fields and sheet actions are 6px. Chips and actions inside a row or card are round pills, so they read lighter than the primary action. Nothing tappable falls between.
- **The 44 Rule.** Every tappable control is at least 44px tall. Primary buttons 52, fields and secondary buttons 48, chips and in-row pills 44. A 36px round icon button gets a hit area up to 44.
- **The Flat Rule.** No shadows, blur or glass. Layers are separated by a 1px `border` and, behind a sheet or dialog, the screen dimmed to 35%.
- Don't nest cards. Rows inside one card are separated by 1px rules.
