# Colors

Black ground, white type, one purple. Surfaces step from black to near-black to a muted grey; one purple carries intent; three status tints are read rather than looked at. Dark is the only appearance the app ships.

Components never use a hex value. They ask `useTheme()` for a role, and the role's value comes from `darkPalette` in `src/theme/tokens.ts`. The table below is that palette, read live.

## Rules

- **The One Purple Rule.** Purple is for actions, selection and state. It never tints a surface for decoration; a tinted card means selected, yours or held.
- **The Photo Scrim Rule.** The only wash is `scrim` (60% black), as a round pill under white text over a photo. No gradient washes.
- **Status labels keep their colour.** Payment needed and New offer are warning; Card held and Negotiating are the tint; Confirmed and Pass are success; Passed, Expired and Cancelled are grey.
- Don't add a colour. If a role is missing, it's a design decision for DESIGN.md first.
